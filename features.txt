Core Concept

Companies post machine breakdown jobs → Skilled mechanics/technicians apply or get auto-matched → They negotiate, agree, and the job gets done.

👤 Two User Types

For Companies (Job Posters)

Company profile with industry type, machine types they operate
Post a breakdown job (machine type, issue description, urgency level, location)
Browse mechanic profiles by skill, rating, and past work
Auto-match with nearby available mechanics based on machine type
In-app chat to negotiate price and timeline
Track job status in real time (on the way, working, completed)
Rate and review mechanic after job
Job history and invoices dashboard

For Mechanics/Technicians (Service Providers)

Personal profile with skills, certifications, years of experience
Portfolio of past completed repairs (photos, descriptions, machine types)
Set availability status (available now / busy)
Browse and apply to nearby jobs
Receive instant job alerts matching their skill set
In-app chat to discuss job scope and price
Earnings dashboard with payment history
🔧 Key Platform Features

Smart Matching

Filter mechanics by machine type (CNC, hydraulics, compressors, electrical panels, etc.)
Match by location/distance
Match by availability (available right now vs scheduled)
Sort by rating, experience, and completed jobs count

Job Posting

Quick post in under 2 minutes
Urgency tags: Emergency, Same Day, Scheduled
Upload photos/videos of the broken machine
Specify machine brand, model, and issue type

Negotiation & Chat

Real-time in-app messaging
Price proposal and counter-offer system
Share documents, manuals, or images in chat
Quote acceptance button to lock in the deal

Verification & Trust

Mechanic ID and certification verification
Background check badge
Skill test or assessment before onboarding
Review and star rating system
Completed jobs count shown on profile

Payments

Secure in-app payment after job completion
Milestone payments for long jobs
Platform takes a small commission per job
Invoice auto-generated after job

Notifications

Push alerts for new jobs matching skills
Alert when a company views your profile
Job status updates for companies
📊 Admin Panel Features
Approve/reject mechanic registrations
Manage disputes between company and mechanic
View platform revenue and commissions
Monitor active jobs on a map
Ban or flag fraudulent accounts
Analytics: jobs posted, completed, revenue, top mechanics
